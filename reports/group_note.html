ML project · SPY Buy / Sell / Hold

What we're building and why

The grade is for method, not profit. We need no look-ahead, honest validation, and a clear explanation of why the model probably loses to Buy & Hold after fees. Every choice below exists to make that story clean and defensible.

The plan

Each day at the close, we predict what SPY does over the next 5 trading days using about 15 features from price, volatility, VIX and volume. We trade at the next morning's open, pay 0.1% per trade, and compare against simply holding SPY.

Buy · big up move Hold · in between Sell · big down move
features at close t→predict→trade at open t+1→hold 5 days

Key decisions

DecisionWhy
Trade at the next open, not the same closeYou can't trade at the close you used to compute the signal. That is quiet look-ahead. The label measures the return we could actually capture.
Thresholds scale with volatility, not a fixed ±1%With ±1%, calm years are nearly all Hold and crisis years almost never are, so the model could "predict" Hold from volatility alone without any sense of direction. Scaled, Buy means "a big move for current conditions". About a third of labels are Hold.
Train 2006–2020, validate 2021–22, test 2023+, with a 6-day gap at each boundaryRandom splits leak the future. A 5-day label at the end of training uses prices from the validation period, so we drop those days.
The test set is used once, at the endEvery look at it that changes a decision turns it into more training data.
Features built the way systematic funds build themMACD and Bollinger Bands are fixed-parameter versions of trend and mean-reversion ideas. We use volatility-scaled versions, plus VIX and the gap between VIX and realized volatility. They still count as the required technical indicators.
No VWAP featureDaily data has no real VWAP. Our approximation moves almost exactly with price ÷ 20-day average (correlation 0.99), so it adds nothing.
Class weights, not SMOTEBuy outnumbers Sell because markets drift up. SMOTE invents days by blending real ones from different times, which doesn't make sense for time series.
Dummy → Logistic → Random Forest → XGBoostThe assignment asks for interpretable models. The Dummy baseline shows what "no skill" scores.
Hold means "keep the current position"Otherwise the model jumps in and out and fees eat the returns.
Judge by macro F1 and balanced accuracyPlain accuracy rewards always guessing the biggest class. About 0.35–0.40 is normal. Much higher means a leak.

How we prove there's no leakage

  • Features for a day are identical whether or not later data exists.
  • Changing prices after a label's window doesn't change the label.
  • No training label overlaps validation or test.
  • A signal at close t trades at the next open, and every trade pays the fee.
  • Training on shuffled labels drops the score to chance.
